Resources to Learn More About Health Equity

Understanding and addressing health equity is crucial for creating a fair and just healthcare system. Below are some valuable resources to help you learn more about health equity:

  • World Health Organization (WHO) - Health Equity
  • Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) - Health Equity
  • National Institutes of Health (NIH) - National Institute on Minority Health and Health Disparities
  • Robert Wood Johnson Foundation - Achieving Health Equity
  • Health Equity Initiative - Health Equity Resources
  • Kaiser Family Foundation - Health Equity and Disparities
  • American Public Health Association (APHA) - Health Equity
  • National Academy of Medicine - Health Equity

By exploring these resources, you can gain a deeper understanding of health equity and discover strategies to promote it within your community.
