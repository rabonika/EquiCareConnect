Mental Health

Mental Health Overview

Mental health is a critical aspect of overall health and well-being. It affects how we think, feel, and act in our daily lives. Mental health includes our emotional, psychological, and social well-being. It influences how we handle stress, relate to others, and make choices. In the past decade, mental health concerns have risen amongst a variety of age groups.

We are committed to raising awareness and improving access to mental health services. Here are some ways we are making a difference:

  • Providing mental health resources and support through our programs.
  • Partnering with organizations to offer free counseling and therapy sessions.
  • Advocating for better mental health policies and access to care.
