What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to help improve your browsing experience. They are used to store information about your visit to our website, such as your language preference, login information, and other settings. Cookies are widely used by websites to provide a better user experience and to analyze website traffic.

  • Session cookies: These cookies are deleted when you close your browser. They are used to store temporary information, such as the items in your shopping cart.
  • Persistent cookies: These cookies remain on your device until they expire or are deleted. They are used to store information, such as your login credentials, so you don't have to enter them every time you visit our website.

Managing Cookies

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings. Here are the steps to manage cookies in popular browsers:

    5. Choose your preferred cookie settings.
