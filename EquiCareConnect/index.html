About Us

EquiCareConnect is dedicated to bridging the gap in healthcare access and equity. We strive to provide resources and support to underprivileged communities and advocate for social justice in healthcare.

Learn

Engage with communities and health equity agencies to understand their issues and collaborate with professionals.

Curate

Collect data and advocate for the unrepresented, educating them about their health rights and overcoming social determinants.

Collaborate

Partner with telemedicine startups to improve healthcare access and bridge the disparity for these communities.

Healthcare is not a privilege, it's a RIGHT.

EquiCareConnect aims to support families of all backgrounds to access the healthcare that they deserve. We aim to address the inequalities and inequities present in the United States' healthcare system. Everyone deserves to have free access to healthcare, and we will continue our mission to make this a possible reality for all.
