Our Goals with Policy Advocacy

With EquiCareConnect, patients can collaborate with legal professionals who will provide informative sessions and workshops on patients' rights and protections.
We will offer resources and guidance on navigating healthcare policies, insurance claims, and legal issues related to healthcare access.
EquiCareConnect will also organize regular webinars and Q&A sessions to empower patients with knowledge about their rights.
