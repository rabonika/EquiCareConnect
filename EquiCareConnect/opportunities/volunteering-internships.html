Opportunities

Volunteering & Internships

Working with EquiCareConnect offers you a chance to contribute to meaningful projects aimed at improving health equity and access. As a volunteer or intern, you will gain valuable experience, work alongside dedicated professionals, and make a positive impact on communities in need. Whether you have a few hours to spare each week or can commit to a more extensive role, we have opportunities that match your interests and skills. We welcome volunteers and interns from all backgrounds to help us achieve our mission of promoting health equity.

Community Outreach

Assist in organizing and participating in community events to raise awareness about healthcare issues and resources.

Data Collection

Help us gather and analyze data to support our advocacy efforts and improve healthcare services.

Event Coordination

Support our team in planning and executing events that aim to bridge the gap in healthcare access.
